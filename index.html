What Is Piso WiFi?

"Piso" is the Filipino word for one peso. Piso WiFi is a coin-operated internet vending machine, often called a vendo. You drop coins into the box, the machine credits you minutes (or megabytes) of access, and your device gets online through the vendo's hotspot. No monthly plan, no contract, no ID.

Who uses it?

Students without home broadband, workers on a break, travelers, boarding-house tenants, and anyone who just needs a short burst of internet.

Who runs it?

Small shop owners, sari-sari stores, dorm owners and side-hustlers who share a fixed-line connection and charge by the minute.

Why people like it

You only pay for what you use. A single coin can buy enough time to check messages, submit a form or download a file.

How Piso WiFi Works Behind the Scenes

A typical vendo is a small computer (often a single-board computer or a flashed router) wired to a coin acceptor and a Wi-Fi access point. The firmware does four jobs:

  1. Captive portal. New devices are routed to a login page until they have paid credit.
  2. Coin counting. The coin acceptor sends pulses; the firmware converts the pulses into pesos, then into time or data.
  3. Session tracking. Your device is identified by its MAC address, and a timer counts down only while the session is active.
  4. Bandwidth control. The owner can cap speed per user so one person does not hog the line.
Why this matters for pause time: because sessions are tied to your device's MAC address, pause and resume only work reliably from the same device. Switching phones, or using "private/random MAC" mode, can make the vendo treat you as a new user.

What Is LPB Piso WiFi?

LPB Piso WiFi is one of the widely used firmware and portal styles for vendo machines in the Philippines. Its portal is typically opened at http://10.0.0.1, and the owner dashboard at http://10.0.0.1/admin. From the customer side you will usually see these buttons: Insert Money Pause Time Resume and a voucher box.

How to Connect and Insert Coins (Step by Step)

  1. Open Wi-Fi settings on your phone or laptop and join the vendo's network name (it is usually printed on the machine).
  2. Wait a few seconds. The captive portal often opens by itself. If it does not, open a browser and go to http://10.0.0.1.
  3. Tap Insert Money. The machine opens its coin slot and starts a short countdown window.
  4. Drop in your coins one by one. Watch the counter on the page rise.
  5. Tap Done Paying so the credit is applied. Your timer starts and the internet unlocks.
  6. Have a voucher instead? Type the code into the voucher box and press Submit.
Tip: If a coin is rejected, wipe it dry and flatten any bent edge. Wet, worn or foreign coins are the number one reason acceptors refuse payment.

Piso WiFi Pause Time: How to Pause and Resume

Pause time freezes your remaining balance so it stops running while you are not using the internet. It is ideal when you leave for class, step out of the shop, or switch off your phone for a while, without losing the minutes you already paid for.

How to pause your time

  1. Make sure you are still connected to the vendo network and have time left on your session.
  2. Open the portal at http://10.0.0.1 (or the address your machine uses).
  3. Tap the Pause Time button.
  4. Wait for the confirmation. The button changes to Resume, which means the countdown has stopped.
  5. Disconnect from the Wi-Fi or leave the area. Your balance is held.

How to resume your time

  1. Reconnect to the same vendo network using the same device.
  2. Open the portal page again.
  3. Tap Resume. The timer continues from where you stopped.

Do

Pause before you leave. Resume within the owner's allowed window. Keep the same phone and keep MAC randomization off for this network.

Don't

Don't clear the portal session mid-pause, don't swap devices, and don't assume paused time lasts forever.

Heads-up: Many owners set a maximum pause length or disable pausing altogether. If you see no Pause button, that is a setting on that machine, not an error on your phone.

Does pausing "stack" with new coins?

Usually yes: if you insert more coins while a session exists, the new time is added to the balance. If the session is paused, the safest habit is to resume first, then add coins, so the credit lands in an active session.

For Owners: Enabling Pause Time and Basic Setup

  1. Connect your phone or laptop to your own vendo network.
  2. Open http://10.0.0.1/admin in Chrome or any modern browser.
  3. Sign in with your administrator credentials. If you are still on factory defaults, change them immediately.
  4. Find the portal or time settings area (menu names differ by version) and look for a pause or resume option.
  5. Enable it, then set a sensible maximum pause duration.
  6. Save, then test it as a normal customer on a second phone to confirm the Pause and Resume buttons appear and work.

Quick checklist before you call the owner

  • Wi-Fi on, mobile data off.
  • Correct network name selected.
  • Portal opened by IP address, not a search engine.
  • Same device you paid with.
  • Remaining time shown on the portal.

Staying Safe on Public Coin WiFi

  • Treat it as public Wi-Fi. Avoid banking or sensitive logins on any open hotspot if you can.
  • Prefer HTTPS sites. Look for the padlock before typing passwords.
  • Turn off file sharing and auto-connect to open networks on your device.
  • Owners: change default admin passwords, keep firmware updated, and never expose the admin page to the open internet.
  • Be wary of "unlimited free time" tricks and cracked tools. They often carry malware and can get your device blocked.

Tips for Running a Piso WiFi Business

Pick a good spot

Near schools, boarding houses, terminals and busy stores. Foot traffic beats raw speed.

Price with testing

Start near local rates, then adjust weekly. Track coin totals so you can see what actually sells.

Protect the line

Per-user speed caps and sensible session limits keep paying customers happy.

Keep the box healthy

Ventilate the cabinet, protect from moisture, and clean the coin acceptor regularly.

Use pause time wisely

It builds trust, since customers feel their coins are respected. Set a clear expiry and show it on the portal.

Back up settings

Export your configuration so a failed SD card or reset doesn't wipe your setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Piso WiFi pause time?

It is a portal feature that freezes your remaining paid time so it stops counting down while you are away, and lets you resume later.

How do I pause time on LPB Piso WiFi?

Join the vendo network, open the portal (commonly 10.0.0.1), and tap Pause Time. When the button turns into Resume, your time is paused.

Will my paused time expire?

It can. Owners often set a maximum pause window, so resume before it runs out.

Can I resume from a different phone?

Usually not. Sessions are tied to the device's MAC address, so use the same device.

Why can't I see the Pause button?

The owner may have turned it off, or the firmware may not support it. Ask the owner.

Is the portal address always 10.0.0.1?

No. It is common, but it can differ by machine and router. Check the sticker or your Wi-Fi gateway details.

How do I redeem a voucher?

Enter the voucher code in the voucher box on the portal and submit it. The time is added to your session.

Is it safe to bank on Piso WiFi?

Treat it like any public hotspot. Prefer HTTPS, avoid sensitive logins when possible, and log out when done.

I'm an owner. How do I enable pause time?

Log in to your admin page, find the pause or resume setting in the portal options, enable it, set a max pause duration, save, then test as a customer.

Why was my coin rejected?

Usually the coin is dirty, wet, bent or not supported. Clean it or try another, and ask the owner to recalibrate if it keeps happening.
